First Shift

Work a dinner shift in another language. Seat guests at the door, take drink orders, run drinks and food, and handle allergies, spills and difficult guests.

This build

The restaurant is open but no guests have arrived yet. Walk around to get a feel for the floor.

Controls

  • Move with W A S D, the arrow keys, or by tapping the floor.
  • Press E or the button at the bottom to act.
  • The clock runs 5 times faster than real life, so one game minute is 12 real seconds.
